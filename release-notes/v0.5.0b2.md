# roombapy-prime 0.5.0b2

**A beta.** Everything in [0.5.0b1](v0.5.0b1.md), plus: Prime robots of
one account share their logins.

---

## One login for every Prime robot

`CloudAccount` has shared one login between all REST clients of an
account since 0.4. A Prime robot was the exception. `prime_robot()` took
the account's login only to start with, and after that the robot renewed
its MQTT token on its own:
- three robots from one login logged in three times when their tokens
  ran out together, about an hour after the account's login;
- each of those logins used an app id of its own, not the account's.

With `auto_refresh=True`, the robot now renews through the account:
- **A fresh login is taken, not repeated.** When a robot's token is due,
  the account looks at its current login first. If it already carries a
  token for that robot with more than six minutes left, which is the
  case when another robot has just logged in, the robot takes it.
  Otherwise the account logs in, once for every robot asking at the same
  time.
- **The robot's REST client relogs through the account** on HTTP 403,
  like every other client the account hands out.

ha_roomba_plus has used one login per account since 4.3.0b1 and said
then that Prime token renewal would follow with 0.5.0. This is that
step.

## A failed login answers for half a minute

Every login the account makes now remembers a failure for 30 seconds.
This covers its `relogin()`, a REST client's 403, and a Prime robot's
token. Anyone asking in that time gets the same error instead of a new
attempt. Against a locked account every attempt extends the lock, and
robots retrying on their own schedules would otherwise keep it locked.

## For callers

- `CloudAccount.prime_robot(blid, auto_refresh=True)` is the way to get
  a robot that renews through the account. `PrimeFactory.create_prime_robot()`
  with `auto_refresh=True` still gives a robot its own relogin, as
  before.
- `PrimeFactory.create_prime_robot()` takes a `relogin` callback, which
  wins over the robot's own credentials.

## Tested

- 1347 library tests on Python 3.11 to 3.14, 526 for the tools.
- Two robots due together log in once.
- A robot takes a fresh login another one made.
- A REST 403 relogs through the account.
- A failure answers for 30 seconds, and the next attempt follows after
  that.
- Each of these checks fails with its change reverted.
- Not yet tried against iRobot's servers. ha_roomba_plus 4.3.0b6 is
  built on this beta for that.
