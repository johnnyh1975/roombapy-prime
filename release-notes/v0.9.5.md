# roombapy-prime 0.9.5

**Three or more Prime robots on one account get their status again.**

Since 0.9.0, every robot of an account shares one MQTT connection. Each
shadow read kept its two answer topics subscribed until that connection
ended, and AWS IoT allows 50 subscriptions per connection. @levidus's
three robots with nine shadows each needed 54: the broker refused the
rest with "Unspecified error", among them the status watch, and every
robot's status refresh failed.

- Read answers are now kept subscribed for at most eight shadows, the
  most recently read; older ones are unsubscribed after a read.
- A shadow that is read often stays subscribed, so reading it again
  still does not subscribe again (which once left a second read without
  an answer).
- Never released: a topic someone is still waiting on, a persistent
  subscription such as the status watch, or a subscription not yet
  confirmed. An unsubscribe the broker did not confirm stays recorded.
- Writes (UPDATE) are unchanged.

## Tested

- The library's tests pass on Python 3.13, the MQTT tests also on 3.11.
- Three robots with nine shadows each stay under the limit; the test
  fails without the change. A shadow read often is subscribed once; a
  persistent subscription is never released; an unconfirmed release
  stays recorded.
- Reported and checked with a local patch by @levidus on three Prime
  robots; this release takes a different, bounded approach that has not
  yet run on a robot.
