# roombapy-prime 0.2.0b12

Two silent failures made loud. Neither changes what works; both change what a broken install can tell
you about itself.

## A subscription that never left could not be distinguished from a quiet robot

`subscribe()` returns a result code, and it was discarded. When paho reports it is not connected, no
SUBSCRIBE packet leaves at all — so no SUBACK arrives, the wait times out, and the function returned
as though everything had worked. The caller then watches a topic it never subscribed to, for the rest
of its life, with nothing anywhere saying so.

That now raises, and says plainly that the broker never saw the request — which is a different
finding from a rejection.

## An unacknowledged subscription counted as a granted one

The wait for SUBACK gave up after three seconds and carried on. The comment beside it read "better a
small residual risk than a broken library". The residual risk is a watcher that reports nothing
forever and looks exactly like a robot with nothing to say.

Unconfirmed topics are now counted and logged. Still proceeding — a broker that never sends a SUBACK
is a worse reason to refuse to run — but no longer silently.

## Why this matters right now

A tester's live map, mission timeline and every command went quiet together while shadow traffic kept
flowing. All three run over one topic namespace; shadows run over another. Four days went into
looking at the features rather than at the transport, because the transport had no way to report a
problem.

Whatever turns out to have caused it, the next occurrence should be a line in a log.
