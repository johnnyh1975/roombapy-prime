# roombapy-prime 0.8.3

**Commands reach a robot on another deployment than its account, and the
600 series is Classic.**

## A topic prefix per robot

iRobot runs its cloud in several deployments (`v005`, `v007`, `v011`, …),
each with its own MQTT topic prefix. The discovery response names one of
them as the account's current deployment, and until now every robot of
the account used that prefix.

A robot can be on another one. @ecovictor75's Combo 105 (FR) was moved
to `v011` while the account still said `v007-irbthbu`. Its state and
battery kept arriving, because shadows need no prefix, but every command
was acknowledged by the cloud and never reached the robot. Sent under
`v011-irbthbu`, the same `start` started a mission at once.

The prefix now follows each robot, as the Prime app resolves it:

- the prefix of the robot's own deployment (`svcDeplId` in the login
  answer), from the deployment list of the discovery response;
- the account's prefix when the robot names no deployment, or one the
  discovery does not list. The second case logs a warning, as the app
  does: the prefix may be wrong for that robot.

`LoginResult.irbt_topic_prefix_for(blid)` does this, and `PrimeFactory`
uses it. `LoginResult.deployments` keeps every listed deployment, and
`PrimeRobot.svc_deployment_id` the robot's own.

The diagnostics report the robot's deployment next to the topic prefix:
OK when the prefix belongs to it, FAILED with a request to report when
not.

## The 600 series is Classic

A Roomba 694 (`R694020`, tankdeer) and a `R671020` (egold555) were not in
the SKU table. Unknown, they were set up from the account as Prime
robots. A 600 has only the Classic-format shadow and none of the state a
Prime robot reports, so their state never changed. `R6` is now Classic;
no Prime SKU starts with it. Both robots are in the fleet test.
