# roombapy-prime 0.8.2

**Where a reconnect spends its time, and no robot id in the log.**

## Where the time went

@glowpartyy's robot dropped its MQTT connection with `[code:128]
Unspecified error` every couple of minutes, and one resume took 40.6
seconds with no warning between "connection dropped" and "watch
resumed". The log could not say where those seconds went.

The "watch resumed" warning now does:

    MQTT reconnected, watch resumed for $aws/things/…CDEF/shadow/update/delta
    after 1.6s (waiting for another reconnect 0.0s, login 0.0s,
    connecting 0.5s, 0 failed attempt(s) with 0.0s backoff,
    subscriptions 1.0s)

- **waiting**: for a reconnect or token swap another caller had
  already started. Its own failures are that caller's, so they log
  nothing here.
- **login**: a full login, when the token was due at the drop.
- **connecting**, and **failed attempts** with the **backoff** waited
  between them.
- **subscriptions**: the SUBACK check after the reconnect.

When another caller rebuilt the connection, the line says so and how
long the watcher waited for it.

`robot.reconnect_log` keeps the last 20 of these: when, the broker's
reason, and the same durations, in seconds. Counts and durations only,
for a diagnostics download.

## No robot id in the log

The reconnect warnings logged the whole topic, `$aws/things/<blid>/...`,
and the token-refresh warnings named the robot by its id. Those are the
lines people paste into issues. Warnings and errors now show the last
four characters (`…CDEF`): enough to tell two robots of one account
apart. `SubscriptionRejectedError`'s message does the same.

## What did not change

The connection itself. Drops are reconnected exactly as in 0.8.1, and
neither 0.7.x nor 0.8.x changed the MQTT path since 0.6.0. `code:128` is
what paho reports when the connection closes without a reason: under MQTT
3.1.1 the broker sends none, so it does not tell one cause from another.

`RuntimeStatsSummary` now says what the apps do with it. The Classic app
reads `hr`/`min` as runtime hours plus remaining minutes and `sqft` in
units of 100 ft²; the Prime app parses it and discards it. It also notes
@jdmasuta's Roomba Plus 410 Combo, whose `hr` cannot be hours (497874
beside 476 powered-on hours). The value is still passed on as sent.
